Privacy Policy

Orbis Flip

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Orbis Flip (the "Application") for mobile devices. The Application is a minimalist arcade game built to operate primarily as an offline experience.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The developer of this Application does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No user accounts are required to play the game, and no personal identifiable information (PII) is requested or structural database tracking enforced.

  • Offline Local Storage: The Application uses local device cache memory (localStorage) exclusively to save your unlocked levels and high-score speedrun achievements. This data remains strictly on your individual physical device and is never transmitted to an external server.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

To support the development and maintenance of the game, this Application may display advertisements. These third-party advertising networks may access basic, non-sensitive system data on your device solely to serve and optimize ad delivery.

These third-party networks may automatically receive device identifiers, including but not limited to:

  • The Google Advertising ID (AAID) or similar randomized hardware identifiers.
  • Basic device metrics (device model, operating system version, and general locale configuration).

This contextual metadata is evaluated by the ad networks under their independent privacy terms to detect fraudulent activity and control ad capping limitations.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not request, ingest, or store any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with child protection frameworks, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Third-party advertising layers are configured to display contextually appropriate materials.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any structural adjustments will be updated on this page with a revised modification tracking index date line.
